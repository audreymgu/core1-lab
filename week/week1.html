Week 1: September 1st, 2026

Assignment

  • Please make sure you have a laptop charged and with the required software installed as indicated in the syllabus .
  • Read the first chapter of Technopoly. Come to class next week ready to discuss the following questions, supported by direct material from the text:
    • To Postman, technology's impacts are broad reaching. What are some of the technologies he discusses, and how do they impact society?
    • Postman writes that "writing changed what we once meant by 'truth' and 'law'." What do you think truth means to a society that has adopted writing?
    • Postman uses "winners" and "losers" as a frame to discuss how a technology's effects are distributed. What does he mean by this? What distinguishes a "winner" from a "loser"?
    • Why does Postman's commentary on technology matter to us as designers?
  • Watch HTML in 100 seconds , then pick an HTML tag from this list by writing your name next to it. Find the documentation explaining this tag in the MDN.
  • Be ready to explain how your chosen tag works to the class:
    • What does this element tell our web browser to do?
    • Does it have any unique attributes?
    • What are some simple examples of how it might be used?
    • Is there any place this element can NOT be placed?
  • Search the web for four images to combine into a visual sequence. They must all be from different websites. Once you have found them, save the url of each image, and have it ready for class.
  • Bonus (just for fun): try using cd and ls in your terminal to navigate around the folders on your computer. Hint - to go back to a parent folder, just type cd .. (the 'dot dot' means the parent directory).
  • For students whose Figma verification failed, don't worry; we won't be using it immediately. I will reach out to the rest of the faculty about how to get this resolved.
