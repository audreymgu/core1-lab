Week 2: September 8th, 2026

Assignment 1: Questions!

  • Collect at least 3 to 4 questions you have about HTML, and post them on Canvas on this announcement by Thursday at 9PM.
    • There is no upper limit. Ask as many as you like!
    • Focus your questions on something specific—it could be a concept, a tag you found, an exercise you did that didn't feel super clear, etc.
  • Before class on Tuesday, look through the list of questions posted by your fellow students and add a reply or "+1" comment to questions other students have asked that you also have. If some students asked the exact same questions as you, try to find at least one other person who asked something you want to know that you didn't think of.

Assignment 2: Take a Trip

  • Take a journey and document the world around you.
    • It could be a walk, or it could be any activity you find meaningful, so long as it gives you rich material to explore. 
    • You can use whatever tools you like: notebook, camera, sketchpad, even clay.
  • Brainstorm ideas about how you want to capture this experience.
    • DON'T write HTML just yet. Instead, brainstorm ways you could structure this content and lay it out, whether on a single page or multiple.
    • On paper, use sketches to diagram out 4 to 6 different proposals for how you would like to achieve this.
      • Depending on your website structure, you may want to use a storyboard (if linking between multiple pages), low-fidelity wireframes, or a mix of both.
  • Collect all of these materials in a Google Doc for class and submit the sharing link via the assignment in canvas. Bring your sketches to class.
