In Class

  • Mid-Term Check-Ins

Assignment

  • Read two excerpts, one by Virginia Held on the Ethics of Care, and another by Wendell Berry on health. Come to class next week ready to discuss the following questions, supported by direct material from the text:
    • Held explores a range of definitions of care by various authors. What are some of them, and how do they compare to her framing? Do you agree with her perspective?
    • In what ways does our society discourage or distance us from the practice of care?
    • Berry argues that there are things that technology *can't* replace for us. What are those things? Why does he feel this way?
    • How are care, community, and health related to one another from Berry's perspective?
  • Reflecting on these readings and how they relate to your own ideas of care, health, and community. Then, identify one area of your life and one new way in which you would like to practice care. Document it as you see fit. Journaling, photos, notes, sketches, poems, are all valid ways of doing this, though this is non-exhaustive.
