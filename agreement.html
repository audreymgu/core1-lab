Community Agreement

  • As members of this class, how can we support each other in our learning?
    • constructive criticism - feedback to improve, no negative intention
    • be present - concentrating on being here to the class
    • don't take feedback personally
    • not being shy to giving criticism - you also don't have to take the feedback
  • What would make me feel safe, included, and encouraged to participate?
    • time: taking time to get to know people, their learning styles
    • collective conversation: everyone is participating, contributing, giving other people an opportunity to jump in
  • What behaviors do we not want to see in class?
    • people on their phone, not paying attention to their craft
    • overall disrespect - be a decent person, not a mean one
    • unhelpful negative feedback - not offering a direction, not explaining why you think something isn't working
